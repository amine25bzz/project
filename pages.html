Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

This policy explains what information APPIKA collects when you use the store, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect
  • Usage data — which apps you view, search and download, used to power download counts and rankings.
  • Review content — the rating, name and comment you choose to submit with a review.
  • Device information — basic technical details (browser, screen size) used to keep the store working correctly.
How we use it

Information is used to operate the store: displaying accurate download and rating counts, showing your reviews to other visitors, and improving which apps are featured or recommended. We don't sell personal information to third parties.

What you control
  • Reviews can be submitted with just a rating — your name and comment are optional.
  • You can contact us at any time to request that a review you posted be removed.
  • You can stop using the store at any time; no account deletion is required since browsing doesn't create one.
Data retention

Aggregate data such as download counts and ratings is kept as long as the app remains listed on APPIKA, since it reflects the app's overall history on the store.

Cookie Policy

Last updated September 2026

APPIKA keeps cookie use minimal — just enough to make the store work smoothly.

Why we use cookies

Cookies and similar local storage let the store remember short‑lived preferences as you browse — such as your last selected category or sort order — so the experience feels continuous as you move between apps.

Types of cookies
  • Essential — required for the store's core pages and download flow to function.
  • Preference — remember display choices like sorting and language, purely for convenience.

APPIKA does not use third‑party advertising or tracking cookies.

Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies at any time from your browser settings. Since APPIKA's cookies are only used for convenience, blocking them won't prevent you from browsing or downloading apps.

Help Center

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often about downloading, installing and reviewing apps on APPIKA.

How do I download an app from APPIKA?
Open any app's page and select Download Now. The file starts downloading immediately — no account or sign‑up needed.
Why won't an app install on my device?
Check the app's listed category — mobile apps are built for Android/iOS, desktop apps for Windows/macOS, and HTML games run directly in your browser. Installing the wrong file type for your device is the most common cause.
How are download counts and ratings calculated?
Download counts increase each time a file is downloaded through the store, and the rating is the live average of every star rating submitted in that app's reviews.
Can I leave a review without downloading first?
Yes — the review form is open to anyone visiting the app's page. We do ask that reviews reflect real experience with the app.
An app seems broken or unsafe — what should I do?
Please use Report a Problem so the APPIKA team can look into it right away.
Still stuck?

Reach the team directly and we'll help you sort it out.
